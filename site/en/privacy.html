Note Privacy

Last updated September 23, 2026.

Verso has no accounts, no server of its own and collects no usage statistics. It only goes online to fetch the lyrics of the song that's playing and, if you ask it to, to translate them.

What leaves your computer

  • LRCLIB: title, artist, album and duration of the track playing on Spotify, to fetch synced lyrics. It's an open, community-maintained database.
  • DeepL: only if you turn on translation and enter your own API key. In that case the lyric lines are sent for translation, under DeepL's privacy policy.

Nothing else is sent: no identifiers for your computer and no data from your Spotify account.

What stays on your computer

  • The app's preferences (position, appearance, shortcuts), in a local config file.
  • Translations already made, cached so DeepL isn't called twice for the same lyrics.
  • Your DeepL key, stored in the system's password vault (Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows).

Uninstalling the app and deleting those folders removes everything.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Fontshare and Google Fonts. To link to the latest version, it queries the public GitHub API. We use no cookies and no analytics.

Open source
